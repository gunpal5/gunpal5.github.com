Remarks

This class defines constants based on the Gemini models documented at: https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/models. Shutdown and deprecation dates come from https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/deprecations. Constants for models that are shut down or deprecated are kept for compatibility and marked [Obsolete] with the recommended replacement.
